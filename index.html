Where I'm Headed

My goal is to build a career in data science, finance, or actuarial science, ideally one that lets me use all three to help people make better decisions with uncertain information. I am majoring in Data Science and Finance with a minor in Actuarial Science at Oregon State, and what connects them is a single question: how do you reason well when you cannot know what happens next?

I already practice this outside of class. I trade options, which means constantly estimating probabilities and sizing risk, and I have published several iOS apps on the App Store on my own. Building them taught me to take a messy idea all the way to a working product that real people use.

When I did the job scavenger hunt and my skills self-reflection in this course, the postings that interested me kept asking for Python, SQL, statistics, and the ability to explain results to people who are not technical. I am building Python and statistics through my coursework, and communication is the skill I want to push hardest, which is a big reason I am building this portfolio.

My short-term goal is to land an internship this summer in data science, finance, or actuarial work so I can see which field I am best at and enjoy most. Until then I will keep applying, keep improving my data science skills, and keep building apps. Longer term, I want to be the person who can both build the model and explain it to the people relying on it.
